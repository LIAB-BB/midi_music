K.478 strings SoundFont notice
===============================

When bundled, k478_violin.sf2 and k478_cello.sf2 are derivative subsets
containing only melodic bank 0 preset 40 (Violin) and preset 42 (Cello),
respectively, from MuseScore_General 0.2. They are used only for the App's
string accompaniment. The pianist's parts are not rendered by these
soundfonts.

MuseScore_General.sf2
---------------------

Current version: 0.2, 13th May 2020.

This is a scaled-down version of MuseScore_General-HQ.sf2 that replaces some
of the larger instruments to save memory and CPU on older PCs. Instruments
without individual attribution use samples from FluidR3Mono.
